# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

StarForge owners, executives, and authorized management staff use this browser workspace to understand and operate the education-center organization. Access is determined by the authenticated principal, effective capabilities, and branch or department scope returned by the production service.

## Product Purpose

Provide one trustworthy leadership workspace for organizational control, staff administration, learning operations, customer growth, finance, compliance, and executive oversight. Success means leaders can see the state of the business, compare operating units, and complete authorized changes against the live backend without losing scope or auditability.

## Positioning

This is the leadership product in the StarForge family. It shares data and visual language with the staff web and mobile products while emphasizing cross-branch context, governed administration, and decision-quality evidence.

## Operating Context

The product is primarily used on desktop for sustained operational work and analysis, while remaining fully usable on phones for review and urgent actions. Work ranges from admissions and organization maintenance to staffing, departments, learning delivery, finance, approvals, reports, and audit review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The deployed StarForge Edu `/api/v1/` service and its OpenAPI contract are authoritative for records, mutations, permissions, and scope.
- Every management action must be capability-filtered and keep backend validation, audit history, and stable identities intact.
- Cross-branch totals must disclose coverage and never infer hidden or unloaded records.
- Budget, customer finance, and compensation remain separate permission domains.
- The current backend exposes audited teacher payroll; it does not expose a generic administrative-staff salary field.
- Sample-mode and unavailable-service states must be visibly distinct from live production information.

## Brand Commitments

StarForge leadership uses a warm, editorial operational system: cream surfaces, ink typography, restrained terracotta and saffron accents, crisp data hierarchy, code-native icons, modest motion, and responsive controls. Staff web and mobile are role-specific siblings, not separate visual brands.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains the production-connected API layer, OpenAPI action discovery, capability model, organizational workspaces, analytics, formatting rules, and automated tests.
- The adjacent `starforge_edu` backend is the production contract source and must not be contradicted by speculative client fields.
- The adjacent staff web and mobile repositories provide the staff-side workflow and native-platform references.
- No universal credentials, private production records, or unapproved business claims are present and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Backend truth, exact scope, and explicit coverage before convenient-looking totals.
2. Make authorized operations discoverable while keeping dangerous changes deliberate and auditable.
3. Explain financial boundaries and protected data instead of filling gaps with assumptions.
4. Preserve one coherent StarForge family across leadership, staff web, and native mobile.
5. Design loading, empty, stale, permission, validation, and service-failure states as normal product states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic tables and forms, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion behavior, robust text wrapping, at least 44-pixel mobile controls, and usable layouts from narrow phones through large desktop workstations.
